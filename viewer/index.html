What the colors mean

Coverage overlay (dBm) · red = strongest signal, magenta = weakest. Always-visible legend in the map's bottom-right corner.

Scenario card swatches · hue-rotation of the whole overlay. Lets you tell two visible scenarios apart on the same map. The numbers don't change; only the palette shifts.

Terrain tile rectangles · green = HD + Draft cached, yellow = Draft only, blue = HD only, no rect = nothing cached.
